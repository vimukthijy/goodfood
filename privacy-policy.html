Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Welcome to GoodFood - Restaurant Finder. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the GoodFood mobile application handles information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

GoodFood is designed to help users discover restaurants, explore cuisines, view restaurant details, and find directions.

Depending on the features you use, the application may access your device's location, with your permission, to help identify restaurants near you.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address through the app. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

2. Location Information

If you grant location permission, GoodFood may use your device's location to provide nearby restaurant discovery features. Location access is subject to your device permission settings.

You can disable location access at any time through your Android device settings. Some location-based features may not work without this permission.

3. Third-Party Services

GoodFood may use third-party services to provide restaurant information, maps, and navigation functionality.

  • Google Maps: Users may open Google Maps to view restaurant locations and get directions. Google's services are subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Restaurant Data Providers: Restaurant information may be retrieved from external APIs or data providers.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the application may be used to:

  • Display restaurants and available restaurant information.
  • Filter restaurants by cuisine.
  • Help users discover nearby restaurants.
  • Provide restaurant location and navigation features.
  • Maintain and improve application functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services when required to provide features such as restaurant searches, maps, and navigation.

6. Data Storage and Security

We do not operate a user account system or maintain a personal user database through the application. However, third-party providers may process or retain information according to their own policies.

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

GoodFood is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage application permissions, including location access, through your Android device settings. You may also stop using the application or uninstall it at any time.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in application features, services, or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page.
